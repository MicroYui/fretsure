# The 82 certified model tabs, characterised — 2026-08-01

`replay_negative_tabs.py` calls its 1,718 raw-model tabs known-bad by
**provenance** — a language model produced them without a solver — and says so
twice in its own comments, because provenance is not a physical property. Its
GREEN count went 6 → 82 over the month and the set had never been looked at. The
guard's own note said so: *"have still never been inspected"*.

## They are not trivial

Every one of the 82:

```
  13+ notes          8 to 32 onsets         13 to 26 fretted notes
  81 of 82 distinct shapes
```

Not one is a two-note first-position tab that would be playable by construction.
The reassuring explanation is unavailable.

## What separates them is the one thing the model is about

Against the refused and the undecided, on the same population:

| | n | max fret | median fret | open share | **widest simultaneous frame** | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GREEN | 82 | 7.0 | 4.0 | 0.21 | **75.1 mm** | 28 |
| AMBER | 197 | 6.0 | 3.0 | 0.12 | **100.2 mm** | 28 |
| RED | 1439 | 8.0 | 4.0 | 0.11 | **129.9 mm** | 28 |

All medians. **The note count is identical across all three verdicts**, and so is
the position on the neck — so the oracle is not certifying shorter tabs, or
easier keys, or first-position material. It is certifying tabs whose
*simultaneous shapes are narrow*, on a clean monotone separation, in exactly the
quantity `d_max` and the hand-centre window bound.

75 mm is frets 1 to 3 in first position: a shape any hand makes.

## What this does and does not settle

It settles that the 82 are not arbitrary. A model that certified randomly would
show no separation on its own governing feature, and this one shows the largest
separation available in the data.

It does not settle that they are playable. Right-hand practicality, musicality,
and whether the sustain the tab claims is achievable are all outside the check,
and no human has played any of them. The guard still asserts provenance rather
than playability and should keep saying so.

What changes is the framing. "82 uninspected false certifications" was the wrong
description of a set that is substantial, distinct, and separated from the
refused population by precisely the geometry the verifier exists to model.
